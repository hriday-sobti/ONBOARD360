## 1. Project Purpose & Strategic Alignment
The **ONBOARD360 Transformation Initiative** modernizes NovaBank International's end-to-end customer onboarding ecosystem. The initiative delivers an event-driven, API-first, AI-assisted onboarding orchestration platform that balances customer friction reduction with rigorous regulatory compliance (KYC, AML, FATF, FinCEN, FCA).

## 3. Project Scope Boundaries

### In-Scope Deliverables:
1. Redesign of customer digital onboarding application journey across Mobile and Web.
2. Integration of real-time computer vision document quality pre-validation (glare, blur, framing detection).
3. Automated optical character recognition (OCR) and automated biometric liveness verification.
4. Intelligent rules-based risk decisioning engine for automated sanctions and PEP screening.
5. Machine Learning-driven AI Exception Triage service to categorize, score, and prioritize non-STP applications.
6. Unified Analyst Workbench consolidating L1/L2 compliance queues, audit trails, and customer communications.
7. Enterprise Power BI operational command center and executive reporting suite.

### Out-of-Scope:
1. Direct migration of core general ledger mainframe software (interface via established REST APIs).
2. Autonomous AI sign-off on High-Risk AML/PEP escalations (retained under mandatory human compliance review).
3. Physical branch architectural redesign (branch staff will utilize the new web-based portal).
