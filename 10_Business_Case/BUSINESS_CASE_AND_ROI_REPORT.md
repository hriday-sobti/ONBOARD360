## 1. Financial Executive Summary
The ONBOARD360 Transformation Initiative presents an exceptional financial investment profile, driven by the massive compression of manual compliance labor, customer support overhead, and legacy vendor fees:
* **Initial Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)**: **$2,850,000.00**
* **Annual Gross Operating Savings (Base Case)**: **$22,092,276.10 / year**
* **3-Year Net Present Value (NPV @ 8.5% Discount Rate)**: **$49,501,858.44**
* **Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**: **> 200.0%**
* **Discounted Capital Payback Period**: **2.0 Months**
* **Unit Cost per Approved Application**: Plummets from **$67.45** (AS-IS) to **$12.55** (TO-BE), achieving a **81.4% unit cost reduction**.
