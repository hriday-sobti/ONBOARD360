## 1. Stakeholder Impact & Cultural Shift
The transition from a manual, paper-heavy, queue-driven operational model to an automated, exception-based workflow represents a fundamental cultural shift for NovaBank's 250+ operations and compliance personnel:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐        ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│       AS-IS OPERATIONAL MINDSET      │        │       TO-BE OPERATIONAL MINDSET      │
│ • "I review every single document"   │   ──►  │ • "I review complex risk exceptions" │
│ • Manual data re-keying & box-ticking│        │ • High-value compliance investigations│
│ • Opaque, isolated departmental silos │        │ • Data-driven, collaborative workbench│
└──────────────────────────────────────┘        └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. ADKAR Change Management Roadmap

| ADKAR Stage | Target Objective | Implementation Activities | Success Metrics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Communicate why the legacy model is failing (burnout, slow TAT, SLA breaches). | Executive town halls, video briefings from Chief Compliance Officer, sharing employee survey feedback on queue fatigue. | 90% staff survey agreement that process modernization is urgent. |
| **Desire** | Foster enthusiasm for high-value investigative work and career progression. | Role transition workshops: transitioning L1 data-entry clerks to junior AML investigators; reassurance of zero involuntary layoffs. | Voluntary enrollment in advanced AML certification programs. |
| **Knowledge** | Train personnel on the new Unified Analyst Workbench and AI decision rationales. | 3-week interactive sandbox simulation curriculum; standard operating procedures (SOPs); role-specific job aids. | 100% completion of training curriculum with $\ge 85\%$ quiz scores. |
| **Ability** | Build daily operational fluency in handling real exception cases. | Shadowing senior super-users; daily 15-minute operational standups; side-by-side coaching during pilot cutover. | Analyst touch time per review achieves < 3.0 mins target by week 4. |
| **Reinforcement** | Sustain adoption and prevent reversion to offline spreadsheets. | Monthly operational excellence awards; weekly KPI leaderboards; continuous feedback loops into Product Backlog. | Zero unauthorized shadow-system spreadsheets detected by internal audit. |
