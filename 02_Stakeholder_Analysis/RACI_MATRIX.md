## 1. RACI Definitions
* **R — Responsible**: The role that performs the activity to achieve the deliverable.
* **A — Accountable**: The sole individual with final approval and decision authority.
* **C — Consulted**: Subject matter experts consulted for input and review.
* **I — Informed**: Stakeholders kept updated on progress, decisions, and outcomes.
